I present my licentiate thesis, **"Machine Learning for Predictive Modeling and Abstraction in Industrial-Scale Systems,"** at Mälardalen University in Västerås, with Professor José Javier Berrocal Olmeda as opponent. The thesis brings together four papers on making machine learning trustworthy, explainable, and efficient enough for industrial use. It is [openly available in DiVA](https://urn.kb.se/resolve?urn=urn:nbn:se:mdh:diva-76650), and the **[licentiate page](/licentiate/)** has the details.
