I publish "HASCO: A Hybrid AI Simulation Compiler for Semantic Accident Reconstruction" at the **30th Ada-Europe International Conference on Reliable Software Technologies (AEiC 2026)** in Västerås, Sweden. HASCO compiles natural-language accident reports into executable OpenSCENARIO simulations for esmini: across 40 real-world reports, its ontology-guided path produces runnable scenarios in 95% of cases, against 5% when a language model writes the simulation files directly. The work is a collaboration with Prover Technology, Volvo Cars, and Chalmers, and the paper is [open access](https://doi.org/10.4230/OASIcs.AEiC.2026.4).
